# Deadlock Timers Reference

Compiled from YouTube video YHc-NMmPjHg and cross-checked against [deadlock.wiki](https://deadlock.wiki).

## Guardians

- 0 min: 50% damage resistance
- Resistance scales down gradually from 0 to 12 min (not a hard on/off), applies to all damage except Pure Damage
- 6 min: 0% resistance (full damage)
- 9 min: -25% resistance (100 DPS gun deals 125 DPS)
- 12 min+: -50% resistance (killable at full ease)
- Intended kill window starts around 6 min
- Additionally: 10% bullet resist (Lane Guardians) / 20% (Base Guardians), 0% spirit resist

## Walkers

- 0 min: 65% damage resistance, scaling to -65% at 18 min
- 9 min: 0% breakpoint (first realistic time to take a walker)
- 12 min+: easy to take with a decent wave/damage
- 35% bullet resistance, 0% spirit resistance (spirit/sustained-damage heroes, e.g. Seven, Sinclair, are strong vs. walkers)
- Gains extra resistance when multiple enemies attack within 51m at once: 20% with 3 attackers, up to 50% with 6

## Jungle Boxes

- Regular boxes (incl. golden satchels): spawn 3 min, respawn every 3 min
- Mid boxes (room above Mid-Boss): spawn 10 min, respawn every 3 min; pay out more than a center sacrifice on average

## Jungle Camps
Camps only start their respawn timer once fully cleared (any surviving creature blocks it).

- Small camps: spawn 2 min, respawn every 1 min 25 sec
- Medium camps: spawn 5 min, respawn every 4 min 50 sec
- Large camps: spawn 8 min, respawn every 5 min 35 sec (at least one strong neutral each; take 20% less melee damage)
- Mixed-tier camps use the timing of their highest tier present

## Urn

- First spawn: 10 min
- Respawns every 5 min (10, 15, 20, 25...) regardless of whether the previous urn was captured
- If a player picks it up before the next scheduled respawn, that respawn is delayed until the urn is delivered or expires (rather than spawning on schedule)
- Color alternates by lane: first urn always spawns Yellow, then alternates Yellow/Green each cycle

## Rift

- First spawn: 10–12 min (targets 11 min, ±1 min variation)
- Respawns every 7 min after being cleared, same ±1 min variation (roughly 6–8 min apart)
- Spawns on a random side lane bridge each time (no confirmed alternating pattern)

## Mid Boss

- Spawns at 0 min (available immediately)
- Respawn after a kill: 7 min after 1st kill, 6 min after 2nd kill, 5 min after 3rd+ kill (5 min floor)

## Rejuvenator

- Duration: 3 min, or until all credits are consumed, whichever comes first

## Bridge Buffs (Power-ups)

- Spawn 5 min, respawn every 5 min (5, 10, 15, 20...) regardless of pickup
- Duration: 160 sec (2 min 40 sec) — roughly 50%+ uptime if always collected
- May or may not still be active for the first rift fight depending on the rift's exact spawn time (10–12 min)

## Troopers

- Spawn interval: every 30 sec (0–20 min) → every 25 sec (20–35 min) → every 20 sec (35 min+)
- Wave composition shifts at 5 min (Melee Troopers start appearing alongside Ranged/Medic)
- Spirit resistance scales with time: 20% at 25 min, 70% at 50 min
- At 35 min (on/off, not scaling): +50% HP and larger model, instantly

## Golden Statue Buffs

- Statues begin spawning at 3 min; a further group of 8 spawns in the room above Mid-Boss at 10 min
- Statues respawn 3 min after being broken
- Buffs upgrade at two breakpoints: Level 2 at 10 min, Level 3 at 30 min (on/off, not gradual)
- Example (max HP buff): +15 at Level 1 → +20 at Level 2 → +30 at Level 3
- Common strategy: hold a statue buff until 10 min (or 30 min) before popping it for the upgraded tier
